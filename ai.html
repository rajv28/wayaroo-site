Use Wayaroo with your AI assistant

Wayaroo has a connector for AI assistants. With it, your assistant can ask Wayaroo for live facts and give you a link that opens the answer on the Wayaroo map. It is free and needs no account or key.

Connector address: https://app.wayaroo.com/mcp

What your assistant can ask

Toilets near a place: public toilets, which are open now, and accessibility (Australia).

Cheapest fuel nearby: prices shared by drivers, cheapest first (Australia).

Weather and air quality now: anywhere on Earth.

Find a place and Plan a trip: by car, public transport, bike or on foot.

The connector only reads. It cannot change anything, and it does not ask who you are.

How to add it

Claude: open Settings, then Connectors, then add a custom connector. Give it the name Wayaroo and the connector address above.

Grok: open grok.com/connectors, choose New Connector, then Custom, and enter the connector address above.

Other assistants: any assistant or tool that supports remote MCP servers (the Model Context Protocol, "Streamable HTTP") can use the same address. No sign-in is needed.

Try these

"Find a public toilet that is open now near Flinders Street Station."
"Where is the cheapest U91 near Point Cook?"
"Plan a trip from Southern Cross to Melbourne Zoo by tram."

Limits and privacy

The place you ask about is sent to Wayaroo to answer the question and is not stored with anything about you. Fair use limits apply. Information comes from open data and can be late or wrong; always follow signs and official sources. See Privacy and Data credits.
