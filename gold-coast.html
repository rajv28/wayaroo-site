Gold Coast trams and buses, live on one map

Wayaroo shows the light rail trams and the buses of the Gold Coast moving on the map, from Translink's open live feed. Tap one to see its route, where it is going and its next stops.

What you can do

  • See your ride now. Tap a vehicle to see its route, where it is going, its next stops and, when the operator sends a prediction, how late it is.
  • Follow it. "Ride along" keeps the map on one vehicle.
  • Nearest stops. The For you tab lists your nearest stops and stations with the next departures in minutes.
  • Weather on the map. Rain and wind moving on the map, today's forecast and UV.
  • Public toilets. Australia's public toilets on the map, with hours and accessibility.

Plan the trip

In Queensland, Wayaroo plans the trip by car, bike or on foot, and shows the live vehicles and the next departures at each stop. Trip plans by public transport are in Victoria and New South Wales only, for now.

The fare deal Wayaroo shows here: 50-cent fares on all Translink trains, trams, buses and ferries in Queensland (permanent). (checked on 2 November 2026; the app links to the source.)

Questions people ask

Is there a live map of the Gold Coast trams?

Yes. Wayaroo shows the Gold Coast's trams and buses moving on one map. It is free and needs no account.

Does it show when the next tram comes?

Yes. Tap a station, or open the For you tab, to see the next departures in minutes.

Where does the data come from?

From Translink's open live feed, published by the Queensland Government. Wayaroo is independent and Translink does not endorse it. Always check the sign at the stop.

Is Wayaroo free?

Yes. Wayaroo is free and needs no account. Your settings stay on your phone.
