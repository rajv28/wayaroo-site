Gold Coast: live public transport is paused

Since 16 November 2026, Wayaroo does not show Translink's buses, trains, trams and ferries in Queensland. Translink's open-data terms ask each app to give the State a release and indemnity, and to ask the same of its users. Wayaroo does not give that promise. Queensland comes back on the map if Translink allows the use of its data without it.

In Gold Coast, Wayaroo still gives directions by car, bike or on foot, public toilets, the weather and places near you.

For timetables and live times in Queensland, use Translink's own website and app.
