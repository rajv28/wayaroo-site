Toyama trams, live on the map

Wayaroo shows the trams of Toyama (富山) moving on the map, from Toyama Chihō Railway's open live data (富山地方鉄道): the City Line (市内軌道線), the Toyama Port Line (富山港線) and the Loop Line (環状線). Toyama City's Maidohaya community buses (まいどはやバス) are on the map too. Tap a tram to see its line and where it is going.

Questions people ask

Is there a live tram map of Toyama?

Yes. Wayaroo shows Toyama's trams moving on a map, with Toyama City's Maidohaya buses. It is free and needs no account. Open it in a browser or get the iPhone app.

Which tram lines are on the map?

The City Line, the Toyama Port Line and the Loop Line of Toyama Chihō Railway. When it runs, the Manyosen tram between Takaoka and Imizu is on the map too.

Where does the data come from?

From the open data of Toyama Chihō Railway, Toyama City and Manyosen. Wayaroo is independent and the operators do not endorse it. Always check the sign at the stop.

Is Wayaroo free?

Yes. Wayaroo is free and needs no account. Your settings stay on your phone.
