Edmonton buses, live on the map

Wayaroo shows Edmonton Transit Service (ETS) buses moving on the map, from the City of Edmonton's open live data. Tap a bus to see its route, where it is going, its next stops and how late it is.

What you can do

  • See your ride now. Tap a vehicle to see its route, where it is going, its next stops and, when the operator sends a prediction, how late it is.
  • Follow it. "Ride along" keeps the map on one vehicle.
  • How late, stop by stop. The next stops show the predicted time from ETS's own data.

Plan the trip

In Canada, Wayaroo plans the trip by car, bike or on foot, and shows the live buses with their next stops. Trip plans by public transport are in the Australian states of Victoria and New South Wales only, for now.

Questions people ask

Is there a live bus map of Edmonton?

Yes. Wayaroo shows ETS buses moving on a map. It is free and needs no account. Open it in a browser or get the iPhone app.

Can I see how late my bus is?

Yes. Tap the bus. Wayaroo shows its next stops with the predicted times from ETS's data.

Where does the data come from?

From the City of Edmonton's open data. Wayaroo is independent and the City of Edmonton does not endorse it. Always check the sign at the stop.

Is Wayaroo free?

Yes. Wayaroo is free and needs no account. Your settings stay on your phone.
