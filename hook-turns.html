How to do a hook turn in Melbourne

Written by the Wayaroo team, Melbourne. Updated 20 November 2026.

Visitors to Melbourne are often surprised by a sign that says "Right turn from left only". At these intersections in the city centre, you turn right from the left lane. This is a hook turn. It looks strange the first time, but it is simple, and it exists for a good reason: it keeps the tram tracks in the middle of the road clear.

Why Melbourne has hook turns

Melbourne has the largest tram network in the world, and many trams run in the middle of the road. If cars waited in the centre of the intersection to turn right, they would block the trams. In a hook turn, cars wait at the left side of the intersection instead, so trams and through traffic keep moving.

Where you must do one

You must do a hook turn where a sign says "Right turn from left only". Most of these intersections are in the Melbourne CBD and on a few nearby streets with trams. At an intersection with no such sign, a normal right turn applies. Cyclists may do a hook turn at most intersections, unless a sign says no.

How to do it, step by step

  1. Approach in the left lane and turn on your right indicator.
  2. When your light is green, drive forward into the intersection and stay as near as you can to the left side. Keep clear of any marked pedestrian crossing.
  3. Stop at the far left of the intersection, with your car pointing straight ahead and your right indicator still on. Let the traffic behind you go straight past on your right.
  4. Wait for the lights on the street you are turning into to change to green. Look at the traffic lights for the road on your right, not at your own lights.
  5. When they turn green, turn right into the street. You go before the cars that wait on that street, because you are already in the intersection.

If the lights change to yellow or red while you wait, that is normal: you still wait for the green on the street you are turning into. Do not turn while trams or cars are still coming through.

Common mistakes

  • Turning right from the right lane where the sign says left only: this blocks the trams and you can be fined.
  • Turning on your own green. In step 4 you wait for the other street's green.
  • Stopping across the pedestrian crossing. Stop clear of it, as far left and forward as is safe.
