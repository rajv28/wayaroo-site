What you can do

  • See your ride now. Tap a vehicle to see its route, where it is going, its next stops and, when the operator sends a prediction, how late it is.
  • Follow it. "Ride along" keeps the map on one vehicle.
  • Nearest stops. The For you tab lists your nearest stops with the next departures. A time from the timetable only is marked "timetable times (not live)".
  • Weather on the map. Rain and wind moving on the map, today's forecast and UV.
  • Public toilets. Australia's public toilets on the map, with hours and accessibility.

Plan the trip

In South Australia, Wayaroo plans the trip by car, bike or on foot, and shows the live vehicles and the next departures at each stop. Trip plans by public transport are in Victoria and New South Wales only, for now.

Questions people ask

Is there a live bus tracker for Adelaide?

Yes. Wayaroo shows Adelaide's buses, trains and trams moving on one map. It is free and needs no account.

Does it show when my bus comes?

Yes. Open the For you tab to see your nearest stops with the next departures.

Where does the data come from?

From Adelaide Metro's open live feed. Wayaroo is independent and Adelaide Metro does not endorse it. Always check the sign at the stop.

Is Wayaroo free?

Yes. Wayaroo is free and needs no account. Your settings stay on your phone.
