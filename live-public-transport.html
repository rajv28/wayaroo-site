Where is my bus? See every bus, train and tram live on the map.

Wayaroo shows public transport moving on the map in real time, straight from the transport operators' own live feeds. Tap a vehicle to see where it started, where it's going, how late it is, and the next stops with predicted times.

Melbourne and Victoria: Metro trains, Yarra Trams and every bus route, from Transport Victoria's live feed. Sydney and New South Wales: Sydney Trains, Metro, buses, ferries, light rail and NSW TrainLink. Elsewhere: hundreds of cities worldwide through the operators' open GTFS feeds.

Nearest stops and next departures, in one tap

Open the For you tab and your closest stops are at the top: train, tram and bus, with the walk in metres and the next departures at each ("605 → A'Beckett St: in 2, 11 and 29 min"). Tap a stop to follow that vehicle live. No searching, no timetables to read.

Is it usually late? Is it usually full?

Wayaroo learns, from the live feeds, how late each route usually runs at each stop at that time of the week, and shows it beside the predicted time ("usually 4 min late"). Riders can tap This one is full when they can't get on; once it has happened on a few different days, the route is marked often full at this time, so you can leave a few minutes earlier.

Your stop, in your language

Riding along, Wayaroo tells you when your stop is next and can announce every stop out loud in any of its 20 languages. New to the city or not confident in English? Show the driver opens a card that asks the driver, in English, to tell you when you reach your stop.

Will I make my connection?

The trip planner shows the spare minutes at every change. A tight change is flagged, with the next trip you could fall back on. Buses replacing trains are marked, and a step-free trips only switch keeps to lifts and ramps.

Fares included

In Melbourne, every trip shows the Myki fare, full and concession, including the current half-price fares, so you know the cost before you tap on.
