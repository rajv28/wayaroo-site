Kumamoto buses, live on one map

Wayaroo shows the buses of Kumamoto (熊本) moving on the map, from the open live data of four bus companies: Kumamoto City Bus (熊本都市バス), Sanko Bus (産交バス), Kumamoto Electric Railway Bus (熊本電鉄バス) and Kumamoto Bus (熊本バス). Tap a bus to see its route, where it is going, its next stops and how late it is.

What you can do

  • See your ride now. Tap a vehicle to see its route, where it is going, its next stops and, when the operator sends a prediction, how late it is.
  • Follow it. "Ride along" keeps the map on one vehicle.
  • In Japanese. Wayaroo works in Japanese (日本語) and 19 other languages. Stop and route names show as the bus companies write them.
  • How late, stop by stop. The next stops show the predicted time from the bus company's own data.

Plan the trip

In Japan, Wayaroo plans the trip by car, bike or on foot, and shows the live buses with their next stops. Trip plans by public transport are in Victoria and New South Wales only, for now.

Questions people ask

Is there a live bus map of Kumamoto?

Yes. Wayaroo shows the buses of four Kumamoto bus companies moving on one map. It is free and needs no account. Open it in a browser or get the iPhone app.

Can I see how late my bus is?

Yes. Tap the bus. Wayaroo shows its next stops with the predicted times from the bus company's data.

Where does the data come from?

From the open data that Kumamoto City Bus, Sanko Bus, Kumamoto Electric Railway Bus and Kumamoto Bus publish. Wayaroo is independent and the bus companies do not endorse it. Always check the sign at the stop.

Is Wayaroo free?

Yes. Wayaroo is free and needs no account. Your settings stay on your phone.
