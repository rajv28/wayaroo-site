About Wayaroo

Written by the Wayaroo team, Melbourne. Updated 20 November 2026.

Wayaroo is a free map app for getting around Australia: live trains, trams, buses and ferries, fuel prices, public toilets, weather, speed limits and turn-by-turn driving, in 20 languages. It is made in Melbourne, for the questions people ask every day on the road and at the stop.

What we believe

  • No account. You can use every part of Wayaroo without signing up. Your settings stay on your phone.
  • Say where the data comes from. Every card shows if a fact is official, from other people, or an estimate, and how old it is.
  • Safe while driving. While you drive, Wayaroo asks nothing and needs no touches.
  • Your language. 20 languages checked by people, and more by machine translation.

Where the data comes from

Live vehicles come from the open data of transport agencies, for example Transport Victoria and Transport for NSW. Official fuel prices come from the Victorian, NSW, Tasmanian and Western Australian governments. Public toilets come from the National Public Toilet Map. The base map is OpenStreetMap. Weather is from the US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration. The full list, with licences, is on the data credits page.

How Wayaroo is paid for

Wayaroo is free. It is paid for by a small number of ads on this website and in the app's More menu, and by labelled partner links. We never sell your data. Details are in the privacy policy.
