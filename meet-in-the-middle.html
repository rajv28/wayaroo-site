Meet in the middle, fairly.

The middle of the map is not the fair place to meet: one friend has a freeway, another has three sets of lights and a river. Wayaroo uses real travel times by car, bike or on foot, and finds the place where nobody travels much longer than anyone else.

Try it now in your browser

How it works

  1. Add the people. Yourself, your Family & friends group in one tap, or a tap on the map for anyone else. Tap 🚗 to change how each person travels: car, bike or walking.
  2. Choose a kind of place: café, restaurant, park, fuel, shopping centre, or pub.
  3. Tap "Find the fair spot". Wayaroo shows the five fairest places with each person's travel time, for example "Raj 14 min · Sam 16 min · Mia 15 min". For fuel, it shows the price too.
  4. Share it. Send the link in any chat. A friend without the app opens it in the browser, adds themselves, and everyone sees the new fair spot.

A fair stop on the way

Two cars from different suburbs heading to the same place? Add where you all go after. Wayaroo then picks the stop where the last one to arrive at the end gets there soonest: a coffee, a fuel stop or a park on the way, not a detour.

Private by design

  • No account needed.
  • The places come with the question and go with the answer: Wayaroo's server does not keep them.
  • A shared link holds each person's place to about 100 metres, not their front door, and only the people you send it to see it.
