Mie buses, live on one map

Wayaroo shows Mie Kotsu (三重交通) buses moving on the map in Yokkaichi, Kuwana, Tsu, Matsusaka, Ise, Shima, Iga and the south (Nanki), with Ise City's Okage community bus (おかげバス). Tap a bus to see where it is going and its next stops.

Plan the trip

In Japan, Wayaroo plans the trip by car, bike or on foot, and shows the live buses with their next stops. Trip plans by public transport are in the Australian states of Victoria and New South Wales only, for now.

Questions people ask

Is there a live map of Mie Kotsu buses?

Yes. Wayaroo shows Mie Kotsu buses moving on one map, from Kuwana and Yokkaichi to Ise, Shima and Iga. It is free and needs no account. Open it in a browser or get the iPhone app.

Can I see the buses to Ise Jingu?

Wayaroo shows the buses of Mie Kotsu's Ise office and Ise City's Okage bus on the map. Tap a bus to see where it is going.

Where does the data come from?

From the open data that Mie Kotsu and Ise City publish. Wayaroo is independent and they do not endorse it. Always check the sign at the stop.
